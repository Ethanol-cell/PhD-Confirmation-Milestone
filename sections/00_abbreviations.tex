\section*{List of Abbreviations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{List of Abbreviations}

This list gives the expansion and project-specific meaning of abbreviations
used throughout the report. Method names such as J1 and stage labels such as E1
are included because they function like abbreviations in the technical
discussion.

AAP & Adaptive average pooling; converts feature maps to a fixed spatial grid before relational comparison.\\ \midrule
ACE & Automatic Concept-based Explanations; a method for discovering and testing candidate visual concepts.\\ \midrule
AC-CTCS & Affinity-coordinate channel teacher-coordinate supervision; an earlier hierarchical distillation line from which J1 developed.\\ \midrule
AUPRC & Area under the precision--recall curve; a threshold-free ranking metric used for foreground localization.\\ \midrule
BCE & Binary cross-entropy; compares each teacher hyperplane bit with the corresponding student logit.\\ \midrule
CAM & Class activation mapping; a family of spatial attribution methods constructed from convolutional features.\\ \midrule
CAT-KD & Class Attention Transfer knowledge distillation; transfers class-attention maps from teacher to student.\\ \midrule
CDF & Cumulative distribution function; used to calibrate a raw score to a display range without changing its ranking.\\ \midrule
CE & Cross-entropy; the supervised classification loss that anchors J1 to the task labels.\\ \midrule
CI & Confidence interval; an uncertainty range for an estimated metric or paired difference.\\ \midrule
CKA & Centred kernel alignment; a representation-similarity measure used to compare layers or models.\\ \midrule
CNN & Convolutional neural network; the main architecture family studied in the completed work.\\ \midrule
DGRP & Dataset-global robust prototype; the geometric median of a dataset-wide spatial-feature distribution or one of its modes.\\ \midrule
E1 & Early relational state 1; the selected early J1 tap at which spatial affinities are transferred.\\ \midrule
ECE & Expected calibration error; summarizes disagreement between confidence and empirical accuracy.\\ \midrule
F1 & Harmonic mean of precision and recall; boundary F1 is used to assess contour alignment.\\ \midrule
FSP & Flow of Solution Procedure; a knowledge-distillation method based on relations between consecutive feature states.\\ \midrule
IoU & Intersection over union; overlap between a selected explanation region and an object mask.\\ \midrule
J1 & Project name for the final selective hierarchical geometry-distillation candidate.\\ \midrule
J32 & J1's joint, variance-balanced principal-component coordinate over PC1--32.\\ \midrule
K-DGRP & Adaptive K Dataset-Global Robust Prototypes; the method selects $K$ robust feature-space modes and scores distance to the nearest mode.\\ \midrule
KD & Knowledge distillation; training a student model using knowledge supplied by a teacher model.\\ \midrule
KL & Kullback--Leibler divergence; commonly used to match teacher and student predictive distributions.\\ \midrule
LIME & Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations; fits a local surrogate around one prediction.\\ \midrule
LRP & Layer-wise relevance propagation; redistributes a prediction backward through a network.\\ \midrule
MGD & Masked Generative Distillation; reconstructs teacher features from masked student features.\\ \midrule
NLL & Negative log-likelihood; evaluates the probability assigned to the correct class.\\ \midrule
OFD & Overhaul of Feature Distillation; a selective feature-matching knowledge-distillation method.\\ \midrule
PC & Principal component; an orthogonal direction of variance obtained through PCA.\\ \midrule
PCA & Principal component analysis; an orthogonal variance decomposition of a feature distribution.\\ \midrule
PCA-RGB & PCA red--green--blue rendering; maps the first three PCA coordinates to colour channels for visualization.\\ \midrule
RGB & Red, green, and blue; the three display channels used in PCA-RGB.\\ \midrule
RISE & Randomized Input Sampling for Explanation; estimates importance from many randomized input masks.\\ \midrule
RQ & Research question; RQ1--RQ4 identify the four questions that organize the PhD programme.\\ \midrule
RMS & Root mean square; the scale used to balance PCA coordinate bands in J1.\\ \midrule
ROAR & RemOve And Retrain; evaluates attribution by removing selected information and retraining the model.\\ \midrule
SHAP & SHapley Additive exPlanations; an additive feature-attribution framework based on Shapley values.\\ \midrule
SimKD & Knowledge distillation with a reused teacher classifier; aligns student features to the teacher's classifier.\\ \midrule
SVCCA & Singular-vector canonical correlation analysis; compares neural representation subspaces.\\ \midrule
TCAV & Testing with Concept Activation Vectors; measures sensitivity to a user-defined concept direction.\\ \midrule
TCS & Teacher-coordinate supervision; transfers teacher geometry through frozen coordinate partitions rather than exact feature regression.\\ \midrule
ViT & Vision Transformer; represents an image as patch tokens processed by transformer blocks.\\
