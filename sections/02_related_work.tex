\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Knowledge distillation.}
Classical knowledge distillation trains a student to reproduce the softened
class distribution of a teacher \cite{hinton2015distilling}. This distribution
contains relative evidence for non-target classes, often called dark knowledge.

\paragraph{Intermediate representation transfer.}
FitNets introduced hints from intermediate teacher layers
\cite{romero2015fitnets}. Later approaches match attention, correlations, or
contrastive relationships instead of requiring direct feature equality
\cite{zagoruyko2017paying,tian2020contrastive}. Our dummy objective follows this
general principle but focuses on pairwise spatial geometry.
